Meme exit rules (exit on any one)

  • Hard stop at −20% from entry (−15% for small caps). Take profit in thirds at +25%, +50% and +100%, and move the stop to break-even after the first third.
  • A dev or team wallet sells or sends tokens to an exchange. The top-10 holders' share rises. Liquidity is pulled. Mint or freeze authority hasn't been revoked. The holder count starts falling.
  • Volume falls by half or more for several days: sell into strength, don't wait for the chart.
  • Macro gate: if SOL closes below ~$85–88 or BTC closes below $73.2k, cut Solana meme exposure by at least half.

When to sell: rules for what you already hold

I don't know which stocks or coins you currently own. Send me the list and I'll add exact exit levels for each one. Until then, these are standard practitioner rules the agents agreed on.

Stocks

  • Set the stop before you buy: 7–8% below entry. Never average down on a loser. A TFSA loss isn't tax-deductible, so the stop is your only protection.
  • Once you're up ~10%: move the stop to break-even, then trail it ~10% below the highest close (~15% for volatile names).
  • Moving averages: exit on a close below the 50-day on heavy volume, and always exit on a close below the 200-day. Act on closing prices, not intraday dips.
  • Earnings gap down of more than 5% that doesn't recover by the close: sell. A gap up that later fills: exit.
  • Take partial profits (⅓–½) at +15–20%, or when RSI goes above 75 and the price is more than 15% above the 50-day.
  • Right now: with VIX above 25 and the S&P under pressure, use smaller sizes, tighter stops and earlier profits.

Crypto

  • Buy only when two of these are true: price reclaims a broken level; funding is flat or negative while price holds; whales are adding while exchange balances fall; ETF flows have turned positive for 2+ days.
  • Take-profit ladder: sell 25% at +20–25% (majors) or 2× (memes), another 25% at the next resistance, and trail the rest.
  • Trailing stop: majors exit on a daily close below the 50-day or ~12–15% off the high. Memes exit on a close below the 20-day or ~25–30% off the high.
  • Overheating: if funding runs several times the normal 0.01%/8h for days while open interest climbs faster than price, trim. Most liquidation cascades start there.
  • Macro gate: a BTC daily close below $73.2k (the 200-day) or a confirmed Dec hike means halve alt and meme exposure.

Canada / TFSA notes

  • Wealthsimple charges 1.5% on each currency conversion (non-USD-account clients). A one-month round trip into a US stock costs ~3%, which can wipe out the whole expected gain. For short trades, prefer TSX names or CDRs.
  • The 15% US dividend withholding tax is not recoverable in a TFSA.
  • Ex-dividend dates lower the price mechanically: ENB on Nov 13 (97¢), FTS on Nov 17 (66.5¢).
